### Comprehensive Hallucination Evaluation Protocol

**Objective:**
Perform a high-fidelity semantic analysis of the dialogue to identify genuine hallucinations. You are tasked with distinguishing between **substantive errors** (which mislead or provide false information) and **benign variations** (natural linguistic paraphrasing, synonyms, or "surface form" differences). 

**Guiding Principle (Tolerance for Linguistic Variation):**
Do not flag a response as a hallucination if the core meaning remains accurate, even if the phrasing differs from previous turns. A hallucination is a **breach of truth or logic**, not a stylistic deviation.

---

### Hallucination Taxonomy

1. **Non-factual:**
   * **Definition:** The response introduces claims that contradict established world knowledge or asserts the existence of non-existent entities, events, or data points.
   * **Threshold:** Minor approximations (e.g., "nearly 100" vs "98") should generally be tolerated unless precision is a core requirement of the prompt.

2. **Incoherence (Contextual & Internal):**
   * **Definition:** A failure of "Conversational Memory." The response contradicts a specific fact previously established in the dialogue history or creates a logical paradox within the current turn.
   * **Threshold:** Only flag if the contradiction negates a relevant piece of information. Natural shifts in conversation or expanding on a topic are not incoherence.

3. **Irrelevance:**
   * **Definition:** The response fails to address the user's intent or deviates to a completely unrelated topic, indicating a failure to process the conversational context.
   * **Threshold:** A response that answers the user but adds extra (accurate) context is NOT irrelevant. Only flag if the primary intent is ignored.

4. **Overreliance (Dependency Error):**
   * **Definition:** The dialogue partner provides a false premise or "trap" question. The responder accepts this falsehood as fact and incorporates it into the answer instead of correcting the partner.
   * **Threshold:** Only flag if the responder explicitly validates the error. If the responder bypasses the error to give a helpful answer, do not flag.

5. **Reasoning Error:**
   * **Definition:** A breakdown in the analytical process, specifically in mathematical calculations, multi-step logical deductions, or cause-and-effect chains.
   * **Threshold:** Focus on the "logic path." If the conclusion is correct but the intermediate step uses a different wording, it is not an error.

---

### Evaluation Instructions
* **Holistic Review:** Check the current response against the *entire* history, not just the previous turn.
* **Semantic Truth:** Prioritize the "Mental Map" of the conversation over the exact words used.
* **Clinical Explanation:** If a hallucination is detected, explain the specific delta between the "Truth" (History/World Fact) and the "Error" (Response).

### Required Output Format
Return ONLY the JSON object.

```json
}
```
