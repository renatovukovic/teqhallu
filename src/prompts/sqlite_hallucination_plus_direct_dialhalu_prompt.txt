---

### Hallucination Categories:
1. Non-factual: The response contradicts world knowledge or introduces entities that do not exist in reality.
2. Incoherence: The response contradicts earlier statements in the dialogue history (by A or B), contains internal logical inconsistencies, or provides "factual nonsense."
3. Irrelevance: The response is unrelated to the current dialogue topic or stems from a grammatical/contextual misunderstanding of the previous turn.
4. Overreliance (Dependency Error): The conversational partner (A) provided incorrect or nonsensical information, and the subject (B) accepted/validated it without correction.
5. Reasoning Error: Errors in logical derivation, specifically within math or complex multi-step reasoning turns.

---

### Execution Instructions:

1. Independent Judgment: You are the final arbiter. Analyze every sentence against its historical context and the Database. Use the Initial Prediction as a second opinion, but do not be afraid to override it if your analysis shows it missed an error or flagged a valid statement incorrectly.

2. Focus on Dialogue Integrity: Prioritize how the response fits into the conversation history. A response is not a hallucination just because it is phrased differently than expected; it is a hallucination if it breaks factual or logical continuity.

3. Strict Categorization: Check every claim against the five categories above. Be especially mindful of "Overreliance"—ensure the speaker did not blindly accept a false premise from the user.

4. Decisive Action: 
   - If you detect a clear violation of any category, the result is TRUE.
   - If the response is factually sound and consistent with the conversation, the result is FALSE.

---

### Output Format
Return your final answer strictly in one of the following JSON formats:
