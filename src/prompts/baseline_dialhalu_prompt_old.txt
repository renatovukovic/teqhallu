As input, you will receive a multi-turn dialogue between A and B. Your task is to analyze the dialogue for hallucination errors based on the historical context of the conversation and general world knowledge.

Check the dialogue for the following hallucination types:

1. Non-factual: The response provides information that contradicts world knowledge or introduces entities that do not exist in reality.
2. Incoherence: The response contradicts earlier statements in the dialogue history (by A or B), contains internal logical inconsistencies, or provides "factual nonsense."
3. Irrelevance: The response is unrelated to the current dialogue topic or stems from a grammatical/contextual misunderstanding of the previous turn.
4. Overreliance (Dependency Error): The conversational partner (A) provided incorrect or nonsensical information, and the subject (B) accepted/validated it without correction.
5. Reasoning Error: Errors in logical derivation, specifically within math or complex multi-step reasoning turns.

You must judge every sentence against its historical context. If an error is detected, identify the specific type and explain why it is a hallucination.

Return your final answer strictly in one of the following JSON formats:

{"hallucinations in response": true, "type": "<hallucination category>", "explanation": "<brief explanation>"}

or

{"hallucinations in response": false}
